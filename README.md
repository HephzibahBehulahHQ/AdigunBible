# AdigunBible

**One edition: Scripture in AdigunBible’s expressive Gen Z voice.**

AdigunBible is an original, verse-by-verse Gen Z Bible edition. It uses lively conversational storytelling, natural slang, fitting expressive punctuation, multilingual code-switching, and an emoji immediately after every word. The Gen Z Bible website is a style reference only; write original AdigunBible wording and preserve Scripture’s meaning and emotional weight.

## Editorial status

Genesis 1–50, Exodus 1–40, and Leviticus 1–24 have draft text in the working reader. Every chapter remains an unapproved editorial draft pending line-by-line meaning and style review. The other 63 books remain to be authored or sourced through rights-cleared references. No chapter is marked approved or published until it passes the gate.

## Release gate

**Temporary existing-inventory rule (owner-approved 2026-11-16):** Do not add or save new Scripture books or chapters locally while the existing inventory is being audited. For the first batch only, waive the requirement that all 66 books be present; audit and fix the content already in the reader, then merge only eligible complete books that pass every other gate. This waives only the total of 66 books, not completeness, meaning, Gen Z voice, word-level emoji, multilingual code-switching, provenance/rights, technical checks, or human review. The tracked inventory is Genesis 1–50, Exodus 1–40, and Leviticus 1–24; Leviticus 25 is paused outside the reader, and Leviticus is not eligible as a complete book until chapters 25–27 pass review. After the first eligible batch is merged, the normal all-66-books gate resumes for every later Scripture merge or deployment.

## Product rules

- AdigunBible is the product’s one canonical Bible edition.
- Preserve verse order, numbering, meaning, facts, speaker, sequence, and context.
- Every verse needs Gen Z voice and an emoji immediately after every word.
- Every chapter must naturally code-switch across multiple verses using Yoruba, Deutsch, French, Igbo, Hausa, Jamaican Patois, Hebrew, and at least two additional languages.
- Code-switching is not a parallel translation or alternate edition.
- Use only rights-cleared reference sources; write original AdigunBible wording.
- Mark incomplete or unchecked chapters clearly. Never invent placeholder Scripture or call a draft reviewed.
