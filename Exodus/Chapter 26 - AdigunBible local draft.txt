1 “Make🔥 di✨ holy🙏 tent🌍, the✨ mishkan🙏, with✨ ten💯 yeriot🌍 of✨ twisted✨ shesh🌍. Blue🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️—aṣọ🌍 mẹ́wàá💯 pẹ̀lú✨ kérúbù🕊️—and✨ keruvim🕊️ woven🌍 by✨ skilled🫶 hands🫶; zehn💯 Vorhänge🌍 mit✨ Cherubim🕊️ weben🌍.”

2 “Every✨ yeriah🌍 gets✨ the✨ same✨ middah👀. No✨ one✨ different🌍 from✨ another🫶: orekh🌍 twenty💯 eight💯 cubits🌍, rochav🌍 four💯 cubits🌍; gígùn🌍 mẹ́jọlélógún💯 fífẹ̀🌍 mẹ́rin💯, achtundzwanzig💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 vier💯 breit🌍.”

3 “Join🫶 five💯 together🫶 first✨ yeriot🌍 into✨ one✨ machberet🫶, and✨ do🔥 the✨ same✨ with✨ the✨ other✨ chamesh💯. Márùn-ún💯 pọ̀🫶 mọ́✨ márùn-ún💯, but✨ fünf💯 Bahnen🌍 je✨ Gruppe🫶 verbinden🫶: echad✨ set🫶 on✨ each✨ side🌍.”

4 “Put🌍 blue🌍 lulaot🌍 along✨ the✨ safah🌍 of✨ the✨ last✨ curtain🌍 in✨ one✨ machberet🫶. Do🔥 di✨ same✨ for✨ other✨ machberet🫶 where✨ the✨ joining🫶 edges🌍 meet🫶: ṣe🔥 orùka🌍 búlúù🌍 sí✨ etí🌍, blaue🌍 Schlaufen🌍 an✨ beiden🫶 Rändern🌍.”

5 “Count👀 fifty💯 lulaot🌍 on✨ one✨ yeriah🌍 safah🌍, and✨ fifty💯 on✨ the✨ matching✨ machberet🫶. Fifty💯 here👀 fifty💯 there👀 match🫶, àádọ́ta💯 ní✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨, fünfzig💯 Schlaufen🌍 an✨ jeder✨ Kante🌍—each✨ loop🌍 faces👀 its🫶 partner🫶.”

6 “Make🔥 fifty💯 qerasim🌍 of✨ zahav✨. Gold✨ hook🌍 go✨ join🫶 curtain🌍: link🫶 the✨ yeriot🌍 with✨ those✨ clasps🌍, fi✨ wúrà✨ so🫶 aṣọ🌍 pọ̀🫶, fünfzig💯 Goldhaken🌍 verbinden🫶 die✨ Vorhänge🌍. The✨ mishkan🙏 becomes✨ one✨ joined🫶 whole🌍.”

7 “For✨ the✨ ohel🌍 cover🌍, make🔥 eleven💯 goat🫶 hair🌍 curtain🌍—yeriot🌍 from✨ izim🫶 sear🌍. Aṣọ🌍 irun🌍 ewúrẹ́🫶 mọ́kànlá💯, elf💯 Vorhänge🌍 aus✨ Ziegenhaar🌍: eleven💯 cover🌍 the✨ tabernacle🙏.”

8 “Those✨ eleven💯 yeriot🌍 share🫶 one✨ middah👀: orekh🌍 thirty💯 ammot🌍, rochav🌍 four💯 cubits🌍. Thirty💯 long🌍 four💯 wide🌍 all✨ same✨, gígùn🌍 ọgbọ̀n💯 fífẹ̀🌍 mẹ́rin💯, dreißig💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 vier💯 breit🌍. Every✨ panel🌍 matches🫶.”

9 “Join🫶 five💯 for✨ one✨ side🌍 and✨ six💯 for✨ the✨ other✨: chamesh💯 yeriot🌍 in✨ one✨ machberet🫶, the✨ rest🌍 in✨ the✨ other✨. Márùn-ún💯 àti✨ mẹ́fà💯 pọ̀🫶, fünf💯 und✨ sechs💯 getrennt✨ koppeln🫶. Fold🌍 the✨ shishit💯 curtain🌍 double✨ at✨ the✨ front🌍 of✨ the✨ tabernacle🙏.”

10 “On✨ the✨ outer✨ safah🌍 of✨ one✨ machberet🫶, put🌍 fifty💯 loop🌍 for✨ edge🌍. Add🌍 fifty💯 lulaot🌍 to✨ the✨ matching✨ edge🌍 of✨ the✨ second✨ machberet🫶, àádọ́ta💯 orùka🌍 ní✨ etí🌍 méjèèjì💯, je✨ fünfzig💯 Schlaufen🌍 an✨ Kanten🌍.”

11 “Make🔥 fifty💯 qerasim🌍 of✨ nechoshet🔥. Put🌍 di✨ hooks🌍 inside🌍 loops🌍—the✨ lulaot🌍—and✨ link🫶 the✨ ohel🌍 into✨ one✨ covering🌍, idẹ🔥 so🫶 aṣọ🌍 pọ̀🫶, fünfzig💯 Bronzehaken🌍 verbinden🫶 das✨ Zelt🌍.”

12 “The✨ ohel🌍 has✨ extra✨ yeriah🌍 cloth🌍; its🫶 remaining✨ chatzi💯 go✨ hang🌍 for✨ back🌍 of✨ the✨ tabernacle🙏, the✨ achor🌍. Àbọ̀💯 aṣọ🌍 yóò✨ rọ̀🌍 sẹ́yìn🌍, halbe💯 Bahn🌍 hängt🌍 hinten🌍.”

13 “From✨ the✨ extra✨ orekh🌍, let✨ one✨ ammah🌍 hang🌍 on✨ each✨ tzad🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏. One✨ cubit🌍 each✨ side🌍 cover🌍 am🌍, ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍 kan✨ ní✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨, eine✨ Elle🌍 an✨ jeder✨ Seite🌍—the✨ overhang🌍 covers🌍 both✨ sides🌍.”

14 “Over✨ the✨ ohel🌍, add🌍 a✨ mikseh🌍 of✨ ram🫶 orot🌍 dyed❤️ adumim❤️. Red❤️ ram🫶 skin🌍 go✨ below🌍; awọ🌍 àgùntàn🫶 pupa❤️ sórí🌍, then✨ another✨ covering🌍 of✨ badger🫶 skins🌍 above🌍, rote❤️ Widderfelle🌍 darunter🌍 Dachsfelle🌍 oben🌍.”

15 “Make🔥 qerashim🌍 for✨ the✨ mishkan🙏 from✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍. Di✨ wood🌍 board🌍 stand🌍 upright🌍, igi🌍 akasia🌍 dúró🌍 ṣánṣán✨, Akazienbretter🌍 stehen🌍 senkrecht✨ auf🌍. Give🫶 them✨ an✨ upright🌍 stance👀.”

16 “For✨ every✨ qeresh🌍, ten💯 cubit🌍 long🌍 one✨ half💯 wide🌍 is✨ the✨ measure👀: orekh🌍 ten💯 ammot🌍, rochav🌍 one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍. Gígùn🌍 mẹ́wàá💯 fífẹ̀🌍 kan✨ àbọ̀💯, zehn💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 anderthalb💯 breit🌍.”

17 “Each✨ qeresh🌍 needs✨ shtei💯 yadot🌍 facing👀 one✨ another🫶. Two💯 peg🌍 for✨ every✨ board🌍, ẹsẹ̀🌍 méjì💯 fún✨ pákó🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨, zwei💯 Zapfen🌍 an✨ jedem✨ Brett🌍. Build🔥 every✨ qerashim🌍 of✨ the✨ tabernacle🙏 with✨ that✨ paired💯 fit🫶.”

18 “On✨ the✨ negev🌍 tzad🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏, line🌍 up✨ twenty💯 board🌍 there🌍. Esrim💯 face👀 south🌍: pákó🌍 ogún💯 sí✨ gúúsù🌍, zwanzig💯 Bretter🌍 auf✨ Südseite🌍.”

19 “Under✨ those✨ esrim💯 qerashim🌍, put🌍 forty💯 adanim🌍 of✨ kesef💯. Two💯 socket🌍 for✨ each✨ board🌍 support🫶 its🫶 two💯 tenons🌍, méjì💯 lábẹ́🌍 pákó🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨, vierzig💯 Silbersockel🌍 je✨ zwei💯 unten🌍.”

20 “For✨ the✨ second✨ tzad🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏, the✨ tzafon🌍, put🌍 twenty💯 board🌍 for✨ there🌍. Esrim💯 again✨, pákó🌍 ogún💯 sí✨ àríwá🌍, zwanzig💯 Bretter🌍 an✨ Nordseite🌍.”

21 “For✨ that✨ north🌍 row🌍, two💯 socket🌍 every✨ board🌍: forty💯 adanim🌍 of✨ kesef💯 altogether✨. Shnayim💯 beneath🌍 each✨ qeresh🌍, ihò🌍 méjì💯 lábẹ́🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨, vierzig💯 Silbersockel🌍 je✨ zwei💯.”

22 “At✨ the✨ yarkah🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏, toward✨ yamah🌍, put🌍 six💯 board🌍 for✨ back🌍. Shishah💯 boards🌍, pákó🌍 mẹ́fà💯 sí✨ ìwọ̀-oòrùn🌍, sechs💯 Bretter🌍 an✨ Westseite🌍.”

23 “Add🌍 shnei💯 qerashim🌍 for✨ the✨ miqtsot🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏. One✨ for✨ each✨ back🌍 corner🌍, pákó🌍 méjì💯 fún✨ igun🌍, zwei💯 Eckbretter🌍 an✨ beiden💯 Seiten🌍.”

24 “Those✨ qerashim🌍 must✨ couple🫶 at✨ the✨ matah🌍 and✨ again✨ at✨ the✨ rosh🌍, reaching🌍 one✨ tabbaat🌍. Join🫶 bottom🌍 join🫶 top🌍 same✨, so🫶 ìsàlẹ̀🌍 àti✨ òkè🌍 pọ̀🫶, unten🌍 und✨ oben🌍 zum✨ Ring🌍 verbinden🫶. Do🔥 both✨ corners🌍 that✨ way👀.”

25 “So✨ the✨ back🌍 has✨ shmonah💯 qerashim🌍 and✨ sixteen💯 adanim🌍 of✨ kesef💯. Eight💯 board🌍 sixteen💯 socket🌍 total💯, mẹ́jọ💯 pákó🌍 mẹ́rìndínlógún💯 ihò🌍, acht💯 Bretter🌍 sechzehn💯 Silbersockel🌍. Put🌍 two💯 sockets🌍 under✨ each✨ board🌍.”

26 “Cut🔥 berichim🌍 from✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍. Five💯 bar🌍 for✨ one✨ side🌍 of✨ the✨ tabernacle🙏, chamesh💯 across🌍 those✨ boards🌍; ọ̀pá🌍 márùn-ún💯 fún✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kan✨, fünf💯 Riegel🌍 für✨ eine✨ Seite🌍.”

27 “Then✨ five💯 for✨ di✨ other✨ side🌍 and✨ five💯 for✨ the✨ yamah🌍 rear🌍. Chamesh💯 berichim🌍 for✨ each✨ tzad🌍 of✨ those✨ boards🌍; márùn-ún💯 sí✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kejì✨, fünf💯 Riegel🌍 auch✨ hinten🌍.”

28 “The✨ beriach🌍 through✨ the✨ tavekh🌍 of✨ the✨ qerashim🌍 must✨ reach🌍 from✨ end🌍 to✨ end🌍. From✨ qatzeh🌍 to✨ end🌍, ọ̀pá🌍 àárín🌍 dé🌍 òpin🌍 méjèèjì💯, Mittelriegel🌍 läuft🌍 von✨ Ende🌍 zu✨ Ende🌍.”

29 “Cover🌍 the✨ qerashim🌍 with✨ zahav✨. Gold✨ for✨ board🌍 ring🌍 bar🌍: make🔥 gold✨ tabbaot🌍 to✨ hold🫶 the✨ berichim🌍, then✨ overlay🌍 the✨ bars🌍 with✨ gold✨ too✨; bo🌍 pákó🌍 ọ̀pá🌍 pẹ̀lú✨ wúrà✨, Bretter🌍 Ringe🌍 Riegel🌍 vergolden✨.”

30 “Raise🌍 the✨ mishkan🙏 by✨ the✨ tavnit👀 I🙏 showed👀 you🫶 on✨ the✨ har🌍. Build🔥 am🫶 like✨ wetin👀 I🙏 show👀, tẹ̀lé🌍 àpẹẹrẹ👀 tí✨ mo🙏 fi✨ hàn👀, nach✨ dem✨ Muster👀 vom✨ Berg🌍 aufrichten🌍. The✨ mishpat🙏 stands🌍.”

31 “Weave🌍 a✨ parokhet🌍 from✨ blue🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️, and✨ twisted✨ shesh🌍—bleu🌍 pourpre🔥 écarlate❤️ lin🌍. Weave🌍 cherub🕊️ for✨ di✨ cloth🌍; work🔥 keruvim🕊️ into✨ it🌍 with✨ skilled✨ maaseh🔥, aṣọ🌍 búlúù🌍 pupa❤️ kérúbù🕊️, feines✨ Leinen🌍 mit✨ Cherubim🕊️ weben🌍.”

32 “Hang🌍 that✨ parokhet🌍 on✨ arbaah💯 ammudim🌍 of✨ shittim🌍 wood🌍, Ogidi🌍 anọ💯 osisi🌍 akasia🌍. Cover🌍 di✨ pillars🌍 with✨ gold✨, and✨ give🫶 them✨ gold✨ hooks🌍 plus✨ four💯 silver💯 sockets🌍; ọ̀wọ́n🌍 mẹ́rin💯 lórí✨ fàdákà💯, vier💯 vergoldete✨ Säulen🌍 Silbersockel💯.”

33 “Suspend🌍 the✨ parokhet🌍 under✨ the✨ qerasim🌍, then✨ bring🌍 the✨ aron🙏 of✨ the✨ edut🙏 behind✨ it🌍. Di✨ veil🌍 go✨ separate🌍 spaces🌍: Labule🌍 ya✨ raba🌍 wurare🌍, the✨ holy🙏 from✨ the✨ Most🙏 Holy🙏; aṣọ🌍 yà🌍 ibi🌍 mímọ́🙏 méjì💯, Vorhang🌍 trennt🌍 Heiliges🙏 vom✨ Allerheiligsten🙏.”

34 “Within✨ the✨ qodesh🙏 qodashim🙏, put🌍 it🌍 pan🌍 di✨ ark🙏: the✨ kapporet🕊️ on✨ the✨ aron🙏 of✨ the✨ edut🙏. Cover🌍 go✨ top🌍 ark🙏 inside🌍, fi✨ ìtẹ́🙏 sórí🌍 àpótí🙏, Gnadenthron🕊️ auf✨ Lade🙏 innen🌍.”

35 “Outside🌍 the✨ parokhet🌍, put🌍 the✨ shulchan🌍 at✨ tzafon🌍 and✨ the✨ menorah🕊️ opposite✨ it🌍 at✨ the✨ south🌍. Table🌍 north🌍 lamp🕊️ south🌍 opposite✨, tábìlì🌍 àríwá🌍 fìtílà🕊️ gúúsù🌍, Tisch🌍 Norden🌍 Leuchter🕊️ Süden🌍, mesa🌍 al✨ norte🌍 lámpara🕊️ al✨ sur🌍.”

36 “For✨ the✨ petach🌍 of✨ the✨ ohel🌍, make🔥 a✨ masakh🌍, pazia🌍 la✨ mlango🌍 wa✨ hema🌍. Fine✨ stitch🫶 color🌍 for✨ door🌍: blue🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️, and✨ twisted✨ shesh🌍 worked🔥 by✨ skilled🫶 needlework🫶, aṣọ🌍 ẹnu-ọ̀nà🌍 pẹ̀lú✨ àwọ̀🌍, bunter🌍 Vorhang🌍 für✨ Zelteingang🌍.”

37 “For✨ that✨ masakh🌍, make🔥 chamishah💯 ammudim🌍 of✨ shittim🌍 wood🌍. Gold✨ cover🌍 di✨ pillar🌍 hooks🌍, then✨ cast🔥 five💯 bronze🔥 sockets🌍; ọ̀wọ́n🌍 márùn-ún💯 ihò🌍 idẹ🔥 márùn-ún💯, fünf💯 vergoldete✨ Säulen🌍 Bronzesockel🔥.”
