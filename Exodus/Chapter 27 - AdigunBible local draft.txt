1 “Make🔥 the✨ mizbeach🙏 from✨ shittim🌍. Five💯 by✨ five💯 three💯 high🌍: five💯 cubits🌍 long🌍, five💯 wide🌍—ravua👀—and✨ shalosh💯 cubits🌍 tall🌍. Márùn-ún💯 ní✨ gígùn🌍 àti✨ fífẹ̀🌍, fünf💯 Ellen🌍 quadratisch👀 drei💯 hoch🌍.”

2 “At✨ the✨ arba💯 paot🌍, shape🔥 qarnot🌍 from✨ the✨ altar🙏 itself✨. Horn🌍 and✨ altar🙏 na✨ one✨, igun🌍 mẹ́rin💯 ní✨ ìwo🌍 idẹ🔥, vier💯 Hörner🌍 an✨ Ecken🌍 Bronze🔥. Overlay🌍 it🌍 all✨ with✨ nechoshet🔥.”

3 “For✨ the✨ altar🙏, make🔥 sirot🌍 for✨ ashes🌍, yaim🌍, mizraqot🌍, mizlagot🌍, and✨ firepans🌍. Every✨ one✨ na✨ bronze🔥 tool🌍, gbogbo✨ irinṣẹ́🌍 yìí✨ jẹ́✨ idẹ🔥, Pfannen🌍 Schaufeln🌍 Becken🌍 Gabeln🌍 Bronze🔥.”

4 “Make🔥 a✨ mikhbar🌍 of✨ reshet🌍 from✨ nechoshet🔥. Four💯 bronze🔥 ring🌍 for✨ corners🌍: fix🫶 tabbaot🌍 at✨ all✨ four💯 corners🌍, àwọ̀n🌍 idẹ🔥 orùka🌍 mẹ́rin💯, Bronzegitter🌍 mit✨ vier💯 Ringen🌍.”

5 “Set✨ that✨ reshet🌍 under✨ the✨ karkov🌍 of✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏, down🌍 to✨ the✨ chatzi💯 of✨ its🫶 height🌍. Net🌍 go✨ under🌍 rim🌍 halfway💯, àwọ̀n🌍 wà✨ lábẹ́🌍 àgbègbè🌍 àárín🌍, Gitter🌍 unter✨ Rand🌍 bis✨ Mitte🌍.”

6 “For✨ the✨ altar🙏, make🔥 carrying✨ badim🌍 from✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍. Wood🌍 pole🌍 get✨ bronze🔥 cover🌍, fi✨ idẹ🔥 bo🌍 ọ̀pá🌍 igi🌍, Akazienstangen🌍 mit✨ Bronze🔥 überziehen🌍. Overlay🌍 them✨ with✨ nechoshet🔥.”

7 “Slide🌍 the✨ badim🌍 through✨ the✨ tabbaot🌍 on✨ both✨ tzadot🌍 of✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏. Pole🌍 enter🌍 ring🌍 two💯 sides🌍, ọ̀pá🌍 wọ🌍 orùka🌍 ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 méjì💯, Stangen🌍 durch✨ Ringe🌍 beidseits🌍 tragen🌍—that's✨ how✨ they🫶 carry🌍 it🌍.”

8 “Build🔥 the✨ altar🙏 nevuv🌍 with✨ luchot🌍. Follow🌍 di✨ pattern👀 I🙏 show👀, the✨ tavnit👀 I🙏 revealed👀 on✨ the✨ har🌍; ṣe🔥 gẹ́gẹ́✨ bí✨ àpẹẹrẹ👀 òkè🌍, hohl🌍 aus✨ Brettern🌍 nach✨ Muster👀.”

9 “Build🔥 the✨ chatzer🌍 of✨ the✨ mishkan🙏. On✨ the✨ negev🌍 side🌍, stretch🌍 fine✨ cloth🌍 hundred💯 cubit🌍: fine✨ twisted✨ shesh🌍 a✨ hundred💯 cubits🌍 long🌍, gúúsù🌍 aṣọ🌍 ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍 ọgọ́rùn-ún💯, Südseite🌍 hundert💯 Ellen🌍 Leinwand🌍.”

10 “Along🌍 that✨ side🌍, esrim💯 ammudim🌍 stand🌍 on✨ twenty💯 adanim🌍 of✨ bronze🔥. Bronze🔥 base🌍 silver💯 hook🌍 band🌍: their🫶 hooks🌍 and✨ bands🌍 are✨ kesef💯, ọ̀wọ́n🌍 ogún💯 ihò🌍 ogún💯, zwanzig💯 Säulen🌍 zwanzig💯 Sockel🌍 Silberhaken💯.”

11 “Mirror👀 that✨ on✨ the✨ tzafon🌍: hundred💯 cubit🌍 twenty💯 pillar🌍 there🌍 for✨ the✨ hangings🌍. Set✨ meah💯 cubits🌍 of✨ curtain🌍 with✨ esrim💯 pillars🌍 and✨ twenty💯 bronze🔥 sockets🌍, àríwá🌍 ọgọ́rùn-ún💯 ọ̀wọ́n🌍 ogún💯, Nordseite🌍 hundert💯 Ellen🌍 zwanzig💯 Säulen🌍. Hooks🌍 and✨ bands🌍 are✨ kesef💯.”

12 “At✨ the✨ yam🌍 rochav🌍 of✨ the✨ court🌍, hang🌍 fifty💯 cubit🌍 cloth🌍 there🌍. Chamishim💯 cubits🌍 of✨ hanging🌍 with✨ asarah💯 pillars🌍 and✨ ten💯 sockets🌍, ìwọ̀-oòrùn🌍 àádọ́ta💯 ọ̀wọ́n🌍 mẹ́wàá💯, Westseite🌍 fünfzig💯 Ellen🌍 zehn💯 Säulen🌍.”

13 “The✨ qedem🌍 rochav🌍 of✨ the✨ chatzer🌍 also✨ spans🌍 chamishim💯 cubits🌍. East🌍 side🌍 fifty💯 cubit🌍 wide🌍, ìlà-oòrùn🌍 fífẹ̀🌍 àádọ́ta💯 ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍, Ostseite🌍 fünfzig💯 Ellen🌍 breit🌍.”

14 “On✨ one✨ side🌍 of✨ the✨ gate🌍, hang🌍 fifteen💯 cubit🌍 cloth🌍 there🌍: chamesh💯 esreh💯 ammot🌍 of✨ curtain🌍, shloshah💯 pillars🌍, three💯 sockets🌍. quinze💯 coudées🌍 trois💯 piliers🌍, mẹ́ẹ̀dógún💯 aṣọ🌍 ọ̀wọ́n🌍 mẹ́ta💯, fünfzehn💯 Ellen🌍 drei💯 Säulen🌍.”

15 “The✨ other✨ tzad🌍 gets✨ chamesh💯 esreh💯 cubits🌍 of✨ hanging🌍 too✨, with✨ shloshah💯 pillars🌍 and✨ three💯 sockets🌍. Other✨ side🌍 same✨ fifteen💯 three💯, ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kejì✨ mẹ́ẹ̀dógún💯 mẹ́ta💯, andere✨ Seite🌍 fünfzehn💯 drei💯 Säulen🌍.”

16 “For✨ the✨ shaar🌍 of✨ the✨ court🌍, stitch🫶 a✨ masakh🌍 twenty💯 cubits🌍 long🌍: blue🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️, and✨ fine✨ twisted✨ linen🌍 in✨ skilled🫶 needlework🫶. Twenty💯 cloth🌍 four💯 post🌍 four💯 base🌍, Akwa🌍 ọnụ🌍 ụzọ🌍 ụlọ🌍, aṣọ🌍 ogún💯 ọ̀wọ́n🌍 mẹ́rin💯, zwanzig💯 Ellen🌍 vier💯 Säulen🌍. Give🫶 it🌍 arba💯 pillars🌍 and✨ four💯 adanim🌍.”

17 “Around✨ the✨ chatzer🌍, every✨ ammud🌍 gets✨ kesef💯 bands🌍 and✨ hooks🌍, while✨ its🫶 socket🌍 is✨ nechoshet🔥. Silver💯 hook🌍 bronze🔥 base🌍 all✨ round🌍, fàdákà💯 lórí🌍 idẹ🔥 ní✨ ìsàlẹ̀🌍, Silberhaken💯 oben🌍 Bronzesockel🔥 unten🌍.”

18 “The✨ chatzer🌍 spans🌍 meah💯 cubits🌍 long🌍, chamishim💯 wide🌍 everywhere✨, and✨ chamesh💯 high🌍. Hundred💯 long🌍 fifty💯 wide🌍 five💯 high🌍, ọgọ́rùn-ún💯 àádọ́ta💯 márùn-ún💯 ni✨ ìwọ̀n🌍, hundert💯 lang🌍 fünfzig💯 breit🌍 fünf💯 hoch🌍, cien💯 codos🌍 cincuenta💯 de✨ ancho🌍. Fine✨ twisted✨ linen🌍 hangs🌍 on✨ bronze🔥 sockets🌍.”

19 “Every✨ keli🌍 for✨ the✨ mishkan🙏 service🫶, every✨ yated🌍 of✨ the✨ tent🌍 and✨ court🌍: all✨ of✨ dem🫶 na✨ bronze🔥. Gbogbo✨ èèkàn🌍 jẹ́✨ idẹ🔥, alle✨ Geräte🌍 und✨ Pflöcke🌍 Bronze🔥, all✨ di✨ peg🌍 dem🫶 bronze🔥. Make🔥 them✨ of✨ nechoshet🔥.”

20 “Tell🫶 the✨ benei🫶 of✨ Yisrael🌍 to✨ bring🌍 pure✨ beaten🔥 shemen🌍 from✨ zayit🌍 for✨ the✨ light✨. Pure✨ olive🌍 oil🌍 keep✨ lamp🔥 burn🔥, Man🌍 zaitun🌍 mai✨ tsabta✨, epo🌍 olifi🌍 mímọ́🙏 fún✨ fìtílà🌍, reines✨ Olivenöl🌍 für✨ beständiges✨ Licht✨. Keep✨ the✨ lamp🔥 burning🔥 continually✨.”

21 “In✨ the✨ ohel🌍 moed🙏, outside🌍 the✨ parokhet🌍 before✨ the✨ edut🙏, Aaron🫶 and✨ his🫶 sons🫶 tend🫶 the✨ lamp🔥 before✨ the✨ LORD🙏. Keep✨ am🫶 lit🔥 all✨ night🌍, tangu✨ jioni🌍 hadi✨ asubuhi🌍, Aaroni🫶 àti✨ ọmọ🫶 rẹ̀🫶 bójú🫶 tó✨, Aaron🫶 und✨ Söhne🫶 von✨ Abend🌍 bis✨ Morgen🌍. This✨ chukah🙏 stands🌍 for✨ Israel🌍 through✨ every✨ generation🌍.”
