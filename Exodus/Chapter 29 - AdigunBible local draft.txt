12 “Take🫶 the✨ bull's🌍 dam😭 and✨, with✨ ya🫶 finger🫶 touch🫶, mark🫶 the✨ qarnot🌍 of✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏. Ẹ̀jẹ̀😭 díẹ̀✨ sórí🌍 ìwo🌍; then✨ den✨ Rest🌍 am✨ Fuß🌍 ausgießen🌍, the✨ yesod🌍.”

13 “From✨ the✨ bull🌍 take🫶 chelev🌍 over✨ the✨ qerev🌍, the✨ yoteret🌍, and✨ both✨ kidneys🌍 with✨ their🫶 fat🌍. All✨ dis✨ part🌍 go✨ burn🔥 on✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏: ọ̀rá🌍 àti✨ kíndìnrín🌍 lórí🌍 pẹpẹ🙏, Fett🌍 über✨ den✨ Eingeweiden🌍.”

14 “The✨ bull's🌍 basar🌍, or🌍, and✨ peresh🌍? Carry🌍 dem🫶 go✨ outside🌍 camp🌍 and✨ burn🔥 them✨ there🌍, jó🔥 wọn🫶 ní✨ òde🌍 ibùdó🌍, außerhalb🌍 des✨ Lagers🌍 verbrennen🔥. That✨ is✨ the✨ chatat🙏.”

15 “Next✨, take🫶 one✨ ayil🌍. Aharon🫶 and✨ his🫶 banav🫶 put🌍 dem🫶 hand🫶 for✨ head👀; their🫶 yadot🫶 rest🫶 lórí🌍 àgùntàn🌍 náà✨ pọ̀🫶, auf✨ dem✨ Kopf👀 des✨ Widders🌍.”

16 “Shachat🔥 the✨ ayil🌍; its🫶 dam😭 must✨ zaraq🌍 all✨ round🌍 di✨ altar🙏. Wọ́n🌍 ẹ̀jẹ̀😭 yí🌍 pẹpẹ🙏, Widderblut😭 ringsum🌍 an✨ Altar🙏 sprengen🌍—let✨ the✨ blood😭 reach🌍 every✨ side🌍.”

17 “Cut🔥 the✨ ayil🌍 into✨ netachim🌍, then✨ wẹ̀🌍 inú🌍 àti✨ ẹsẹ̀🌍 rẹ̀🫶: the✨ qerev🌍 and✨ legs🌍 get✨ washed🌍. Put🌍 dem🫶 with✨ di✨ pieces🌍 and✨ the✨ rosh👀, Stücke🌍 Eingeweide🌍 Beine🌍 Kopf👀 ordnen🌍.”

18 “The✨ whole✨ ayil🌍 goes🔥 on✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏 as✨ an✨ olah🔥 to✨ the✨ LORD🙏. Jó🔥 gbogbo✨ àgùntàn🌍 sí✨ Olúwa🙏; di✨ whole✨ ram🌍 burn🔥 there🌍 in✨ fire🔥, and✨ its🫶 reiach✨ rises🔥 pleasing🫶 to✨ him🙏, ein✨ lieblicher✨ Duft🌍 für✨ Gott🙏.”

19 “For✨ the✨ other✨ ayil🌍, Aharon🫶 and✨ his🫶 banav🫶 must✨ lé🌍 ọwọ́🫶 sórí🌍 àgùntàn🌍 kejì✨. Dem🫶 go✨ put🌍 hand🫶 again🌍: their🫶 yadot🫶 rest🫶 auf✨ dem✨ Kopf👀 des✨ Widders🌍.”

20 “Slaughter🔥 that✨ ram🌍; its🫶 dam😭 goes🌍 on✨ Aharon🫶 and✨ his🫶 sons🫶—right✨ ear👀 right✨ thumb🫶 right✨ toe🌍. Touch🫶 the✨ tip🌍 of✨ each✨ right✨ ozan👀, the✨ right✨ bohen🫶, and✨ the✨ right✨ big✨ toe🌍; ẹ̀jẹ̀😭 sí✨ etí👀 ọwọ́🫶 ẹsẹ̀🌍 ọ̀tún✨. Das✨ übrige✨ Blut😭 ringsum🌍 sprengen🌍 the✨ altar🙏.”

21 “From✨ the✨ mizbeach🙏, take🫶 some✨ dam😭 and✨ shemen🌍 of✨ mishchah🙏. Sprinkle🌍 both✨ for✨ everybody🫶: Aaron🫶, his🫶 garments🌍, his🫶 sons🫶, and✨ their🫶 garments🌍. Ẹ̀jẹ̀😭 àti✨ epo🌍 sórí🌍 wọn🫶, Blut😭 und✨ Salböl🌍 auf✨ Gewänder🌍 sprengen🌍—they🫶 and✨ the✨ clothes🌍 become✨ holy🙏.”
