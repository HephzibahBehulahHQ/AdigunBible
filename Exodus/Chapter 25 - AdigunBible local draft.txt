5 “Bring🌍 ram🫶 orot🌍 dyed❤️ adumim❤️, badger🫶 skins🌍, and✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍. Red❤️ ram🫶 skin🌍 badger🫶 skin🌍 wood🌍; Awọ🌍 àgùntàn🫶 pupa❤️ àti✨ igi🌍; rot❤️ gefärbte✨ Widderfelle🌍 Dachsfelle🌍 Holz🌍. Every✨ piece🌍 has✨ a✨ place🌍.”

6 “Bring🌍 shemen🌍 for✨ the✨ maor✨, besamim🌍 for✨ the✨ anointing🙏 oil🌍, and✨ for✨ fragrant🌍 qetoret🙏. Oil🌍 for✨ light✨ spice🌍 for✨ incense🙏; Epo🌍 fún✨ ìmọ́lẹ̀✨ àti✨ tùràrí🙏; Öl🌍 fürs✨ Licht✨ Gewürze🌍 zum✨ Räuchern🙏. Let✨ each✨ fragrance🌍 serve🔥 its🫶 purpose🙏.”

7 “Bring🌍 shoham🌍 avanim🌍 and✨ other✨ stones🌍 to✨ set✨ in✨ the✨ efod🙏 and✨ choshen🙏. Stone🌍 go✨ sit✨ for✨ ephod🙏 and✨ breastplate🙏. Òkúta🌍 fún✨ efodu🙏 àti✨ àyà🙏; Steine🌍 für✨ Efod🙏 und✨ Brustschild🙏.”

8 “Have✨ them🫶 build🔥 me🙏 a✨ miqdash🙏, so✨ I🙏 may✨ shakan🕊️ betokham🫶. Make✨ dem🫶 build🔥 place🌍 for✨ me🙏; Kí✨ wọ́n🫶 kọ́🔥 ibi🌍 mímọ́🙏; baut🔥 mir🙏 ein✨ Heiligtum🙏. Li🙏—I🙏 will✨ be✨ right✨ there👀 among✨ them🫶.”

9 “Use🔥 the✨ tavnit👀 I🙏 show👀 you🫶 for✨ the✨ mishkan🙏 and✨ every✨ keli🌍 in✨ it🌍. Asah🔥 it🌍 exactly✨ that✨ way👀. Follow🌍 every✨ pattern👀 I🙏 show👀; Ẹ✨ tẹ̀lé🌍 àpẹẹrẹ👀 gbogbo✨ rẹ̀🌍; alles✨ nach✨ meinem🙏 Muster👀 bauen🔥. No✨ freestyle🔥 on✨ this✨ design👀.”

10 “Make🔥 an✨ aron🙏 from✨ shittim🌍: orekh🌍 two💯 and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍, rochav🌍 one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍, and✨ height🌍 one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍. Two💯 half💯 long🌍 one✨ half💯 wide🌍; Gígùn🌍 ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍 méjì💯 àbọ̀💯; zweieinhalb💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 eineinhalb💯 breit🌍. Same✨ one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 high🌍.”

11 “Cover🌍 that✨ aron🙏 with✨ zahav✨ tahor✨ through✨ and✨ through✨—miBayit🌍 and✨ outside🌍. Add🫶 a✨ gold✨ molding🌍 all✨ around🌍. Gold✨ inside🌍 gold✨ outside🌍 round🌍; Fi✨ wúrà✨ bo🌍 inú🌍 àti✨ òde🌍; innen🌍 außen🌍 mit✨ reinem✨ Gold✨. Let✨ the✨ rim🌍 shine✨ too✨.”

12 “Cast🔥 four💯 tabbaot🌍 of✨ zahav✨ for✨ the✨ aron🙏, one✨ at✨ each✨ of✨ its🫶 four💯 paamot🌍. Two💯 rings🌍 on✨ one✨ side🌍, two💯 on✨ the✨ other🌍. Two💯 ring🌍 each✨ side🌍 four💯 total💯; Orùka🌍 méjì💯 ní✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨; vier💯 Goldringe🌍 zwei💯 je✨ Seite🌍. Count👀 it🌍 right💯.”

13 “Make🔥 badim🌍 from✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍, then✨ cover🌍 them✨ with✨ zahav✨. Cut🔥 wood🌍 make🔥 pole🌍 cover🌍 gold✨; Fi✨ wúrà✨ bo🌍 ọ̀pá🌍; Stangen🌍 aus✨ Akazienholz🌍 vergolden✨. These✨ poles🌍 will✨ carry🌍 what✨ is✨ holy🙏.”

14 “Slide🌍 the✨ badim🌍 through✨ the✨ tabbaot🌍 on✨ the✨ tzidot🌍 of✨ the✨ aron🙏; use🔥 them✨ to✨ carry🌍 it🌍. Put🌍 pole🌍 for✨ ring🌍 carry🌍 ark🙏; Fi✨ ọ̀pá🌍 sínú🌍 orùka🌍; Stangen🌍 durch✨ Ringe🌍 zum✨ Tragen🌍. That's✨ the✨ way🌍 it🌍 moves🌍.”

15 “Leave✨ those✨ badim🌍 in✨ the✨ tabbaot🌍 of✨ the✨ aron🙏. Lo✨ pull🌍 them✨ out🌍. No✨ remove🌍 pole🌍 from✨ ring🌍; Má✨ yọ🌍 ọ̀pá🌍 náà✨ kúrò🌍; Stangen🌍 bleiben✨ in✨ den✨ Ringen🌍. Keep✨ them✨ there👀, always✨.”

16 “Put🌍 the✨ edut🙏 I🙏 will✨ natan🫶 you🫶 into✨ the✨ aron🙏, sham👀. The✨ word🙏 I🙏 give🫶 go✨ inside🌍; Fi✨ ẹ̀rí🙏 sínú🌍 àpótí🙏; das✨ Zeugnis🙏 in✨ die✨ Lade🙏 legen🌍. That✨ place🌍 is✨ for✨ what✨ I🙏 give🫶.”

17 “Make🔥 a✨ kapporet🕊️ of✨ zahav✨ tahor✨. Its🫶 orekh🌍 is✨ two💯 and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍; its🫶 width🌍 one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍. Two💯 half💯 long🌍 one✨ half💯 wide🌍; Wúrà✨ mímọ́🙏 ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍 méjì💯 àbọ̀💯; zweieinhalb💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 eineinhalb💯 breit🌍.”

18 “Hammer🔥 out✨ two💯 keruvim🕊️ from✨ zahav✨, one✨ at✨ each✨ qatzeh🌍 of✨ the✨ kapporet🕊️. Two💯 gold✨ cherub🕊️ one✨ each✨ side🌍; Kérúbù🕊️ méjì💯 ní✨ òpin🌍 méjèèjì💯; zwei💯 Cherubim🕊️ aus✨ getriebenem🔥 Gold✨. Shape🔥 them✨ as✨ one✨ craft🌍.”

19 “One✨ keruv🕊️ at✨ one✨ qatzeh🌍, another✨ at✨ the✨ other🌍—both✨ made🔥 from✨ the✨ kapporet🕊️ itself✨, joined🫶 at✨ the✨ two💯 qatzot🌍. One✨ here👀 one✨ there👀 both🫶 joined🫶; Ọ̀kan✨ ní✨ òpin🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨; je✨ einer✨ an✨ beiden💯 Enden🌍. No✨ loose✨ add-ons🌍.”

20 “The✨ keruvim🕊️ spread🌍 their🫶 kenafayim🕊️ upward🌍, shielding🫶 the✨ kapporet🕊️ beneath🌍. Their🫶 panim👀 look👀 toward✨ each✨ other🫶 and✨ down🌍 toward✨ the✨ cover🌍. Wing🕊️ cover🌍 top🌍 face👀 face👀; Ìyẹ́🕊️ wọn🫶 bo🌍 ìtẹ́🙏 náà✨; Flügel🕊️ decken🌍 den✨ Gnadenthron🕊️.”

21 “Place🌍 the✨ kapporet🕊️ on✨ top🌍 of✨ the✨ aron🙏, and✨ put🌍 the✨ edut🙏 I🙏 give🫶 you🫶 inside🌍 the✨ aron🙏. Cover🌍 go✨ top🌍 word🙏 go✨ inside🌍; Fi✨ ìtẹ́🙏 sí✨ orí🌍 àpótí🙏; Deckel🌍 oben🌍 Zeugnis🙏 innen🌍. Top🌍 and✨ inside🌍, each✨ in✨ its🫶 place🌍.”

22 “There👀 I🙏 will✨ yaad🙏 you🫶 and✨ diber✨ with✨ you🫶 from✨ above🌍 the✨ kapporet🕊️, between✨ the✨ two💯 keruvim🕊️ on✨ the✨ ark🙏 of✨ the✨ testimony🙏. From✨ there👀 I🙏 will✨ give🫶 every✨ command🙏 for✨ Israel🫶. Na✨ there👀 I🙏 go✨ talk✨; Ní✨ ibẹ̀👀 ni✨ mo🙏 ti✨ sọ✨; dort👀 rede✨ ich🙏 mit✨ dir🫶.”

23 “Make🔥 a✨ shulchan🌍 from✨ shittim🌍: orekh🌍 two💯 cubits🌍, rochav🌍 one✨ cubit🌍, height🌍 one✨ and✨ a✨ half💯 cubits🌍. Two💯 long🌍 one✨ wide🌍 half💯 extra✨ tall🌍; Tábìlì🌍 gígùn🌍 ìgbọ̀nwọ́🌍 méjì💯; zwei💯 Ellen🌍 lang🌍 eine✨ breit🌍. Measure👀 every✨ side🌍.”

24 “Cover🌍 the✨ shulchan🌍 with✨ zahav✨ tahor✨, then✨ run🌍 a✨ gold✨ zer🌍 around✨ it🌍. Gold✨ cover🌍 table🌍 edge🌍 round🌍; Fi✨ wúrà✨ mímọ́🙏 bo🌍 tábìlì🌍; reines✨ Gold✨ und✨ goldener✨ Rand🌍. Let✨ the✨ edge🌍 match✨ the✨ top🌍.”

25 “Build🔥 a✨ misgeret🌍 one✨ tefach🌍 wide🌍 all✨ around✨ the✨ table🌍, and✨ set✨ a✨ zer🌍 of✨ zahav✨ around✨ that✨ border🌍 too✨. One✨ hand🌍 wide🌍 border🌍 round🌍; Àgbègbè🌍 ìwọ̀n🌍 àtẹ́lẹwọ́🫶 kan✨; eine✨ Handbreit🌍 Rand🌍 ringsum🌍. Gold✨ around✨ gold✨, precise👀.”

26 “Cast🔥 four💯 tabbaot🌍 of✨ zahav✨ for✨ the✨ table🌍, then✨ fix🔥 them✨ at✨ the✨ four💯 peot🌍 over✨ its🫶 four💯 raglayim🌍. Four💯 corner🌍 four💯 foot🌍 ring🌍; Orùka🌍 mẹ́rin💯 ní✨ igun🌍 mẹ́rin💯; vier💯 Goldringe🌍 an✨ vier💯 Füßen🌍.”

27 “The✨ tabbaot🌍 sit✨ beside✨ the✨ misgeret🌍 to✨ hold🫶 the✨ badim🌍 for✨ carrying🌍 the✨ shulchan🌍. Ring🌍 hold🫶 pole🌍 carry🌍 table🌍; Orùka🌍 gba🫶 ọ̀pá🌍 gbé🌍 tábìlì🌍; Ringe🌍 neben✨ Rand🌍 tragen🌍 Stangen🌍. That's✨ their🫶 job🔥.”

28 “Cut🔥 the✨ badim🌍 from✨ etz🌍 of✨ shittim🌍, wrap🌍 them✨ in✨ zahav✨, and✨ carry🌍 the✨ table🌍 with✨ them✨. Gold✨ pole🌍 go✨ carry🌍 table🌍; Fi✨ ọ̀pá🌍 gbé🌍 tábìlì🌍 náà✨; vergoldete✨ Stangen🌍 tragen🌍 den✨ Tisch🌍. The✨ poles🌍 do✨ the✨ carrying🌍.”

29 “Make🔥 its🫶 qearot🌍, kappot🌍, covers🌍, and✨ menaqiyot🌍—every✨ piece🌍 from✨ pure✨ zahav✨. Dish🌍 spoon🌍 cover🌍 bowl🌍 all✨ gold✨; Àwo🌍 ṣíbí🌍 ìbòrí🌍 gbogbo✨ wúrà✨; Schalen🌍 Löffel🌍 Deckel🌍 Becher🌍 Gold✨. The✨ table🌍 needs✨ every✨ vessel🌍.”

30 “Set✨ lechem🌍 of✨ the✨ panim👀 on✨ the✨ shulchan🌍 before✨ lefanai🙏, always✨. Bread🌍 go✨ stay✨ before✨ me🙏; Kí✨ àkàrà🌍 wà✨ ní✨ iwájú👀 mi🙏; Brot🌍 liegt✨ immer✨ vor✨ mir🙏. Keep✨ that✨ presence🙏 continual✨.”

31 “Hammer🔥 a✨ menorah🕊️ from✨ zahav✨ tahor✨, one✨ mikshah🔥. Its🫶 shaft🌍, branches🌍, cups🌍, knobs🌍, and✨ flowers🌍 must✨ rise🌍 from✨ that✨ same✨ piece🌍, une✨ seule✨ pièce🌍 en✨ or✨. One✨ gold✨ piece🌍 make🔥 all✨ parts🌍; Ohun✨ gbogbo✨ jáde🌍 lára🌍 rẹ̀🫶; alles✨ aus✨ einem✨ Goldstück✨ schlagen🔥.”

32 “Six💯 qanim🌍 come🌍 from✨ the✨ sides🌍 of✨ the✨ menorah🕊️—shloshah💯 from✨ one✨ tzad🌍, three💯 from✨ the✨ other🌍. Alaka🌍 atọ💯 akụkụ🌍 abụọ💯. Three💯 for✨ left🌍 three💯 for✨ right🌍; Ẹ̀ka🌍 mẹ́ta💯 ní✨ ẹ̀gbẹ́🌍 kọ̀ọ̀kan✨; drei💯 Zweige🌍 auf✨ jeder✨ Seite🌍. Six💯 total💯, balanced✨.”

34 “On✨ the✨ main✨ menorah🕊️ itself✨, make🔥 arbaah💯 geviyim🌍 shaped✨ like✨ shqedim🌍, each✨ with✨ its🫶 knobs🌍 and✨ flowers🌍. Main✨ stand🌍 get✨ four💯 almond🌍 cup🌍; Àwo🌍 mẹ́rin💯 wà✨ ní✨ àárín🌍; vier💯 Kelche🌍 am✨ Mittelstamm🌍. Details👀 matter💯.”

36 “The✨ kaftorim🌍 and✨ qanim🌍 must✨ be✨ one✨ with✨ the✨ stand🕊️—one✨ mikshah🔥 of✨ zahav✨ pure✨ throughout✨. No✨ join🫶 piece🌍 na✨ one✨ gold✨; Ọ̀kan✨ ṣoṣo✨ ni✨ gbogbo✨ rẹ̀🌍; ein✨ Stück🌍 aus✨ reinem✨ Gold✨.”

37 “Make🔥 its🫶 shivah💯 nerot🌍 and✨ light🔥 them✨ so✨ their🫶 or✨ shines✨ toward✨ the✨ panim👀. Fitilu🌍 bakwai💯 su🫶 haskaka✨; taa🌍 saba💯 zitoe✨ nuru✨. Seven💯 lamp🌍 go✨ light🔥 front🌍; Fìtílà🌍 méje💯 tan🔥 sí✨ iwájú👀; sieben💯 Lampen🌍 leuchten✨ nach✨ vorn🌍.”

38 “Its🫶 melqachayim🌍 and✨ machtot🌍 must✨ be✨ zahav✨ tahor✨ too✨. Tong🌍 and✨ tray🌍 pure✨ gold✨; Ọ̀pá🌍 àti✨ àwo🌍 jẹ́✨ wúrà✨; Zangen🌍 und✨ Schalen🌍 aus✨ Gold✨. Even✨ the✨ small✨ tools🌍 belong✨ in✨ the✨ design👀.”

39 “Use🔥 one✨ kikkar💯 of✨ zahav✨ pure✨ to✨ asah🔥 the✨ lampstand🕊️ and✨ all✨ its🫶 kelim🌍. un✨ talento💯 de✨ oro✨. One✨ talent💯 gold✨ for✨ all✨; Tálẹ́ńtì💯 wúrà✨ kan✨ fún✨ gbogbo✨; ein✨ Talent💯 Gold✨ für✨ alles✨. No✨ missing✨ pieces🌍.”

40 “Look👀—raah👀 the✨ tavnit👀 I🙏 showed👀 you🫶 on✨ the✨ har🌍; asah🔥 every✨ thing🌍 by✨ that✨ exact👀 design👀. mek🔥 it🌍 di✨ same✨ way🌍. Make🔥 am🌍 like✨ wetin👀 you🫶 see👀; Ṣe🔥 gẹ́gẹ́✨ bí✨ àpẹẹrẹ👀 náà✨; genau✨ nach✨ dem✨ gezeigten👀 Muster👀.”
