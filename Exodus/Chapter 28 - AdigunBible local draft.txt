8 “The✨ woven✨ cheshev🌍 on✨ the✨ efod🙏 must✨ be✨ one✨ with✨ its🫶 craft🔥: zahav✨, tekhelet🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️, fine✨ twisted✨ linen🌍. Belt🌍 and✨ ephod🙏 same✨ design👀, àmùrè🌍 bá✨ efodu🙏 mu🫶, Gürtel🌍 passt✨ zum✨ Efod🙏.”

13 “Make🔥 mishbetzot✨ of✨ zahav✨ for✨ the✨ avanim🌍 bearing🌍 the✨ shemot👀. Gold✨ setting🌍 hold🫶 stone🌍 firm✨, fi✨ wúrà✨ ṣe🔥 ìtẹ́🌍 òkúta🌍, goldene✨ Fassungen🌍 für✨ die✨ Steine🌍.”

15 “Craft🔥 the✨ choshen🙏 of✨ mishpat🙏 with✨ the✨ same✨ skilled✨ maaseh🔥 as✨ the✨ efod🙏. Make🔥 am🫶 match🫶 di✨ ephod🙏, àyà🙏 bá✨ efodu🙏 mu🫶, Brustschild🙏 wie✨ Efod🙏 kunstvoll✨ weben🌍: gold✨, blue🌍, purple🔥, scarlet❤️, and✨ fine✨ twisted✨ linen🌍.”
