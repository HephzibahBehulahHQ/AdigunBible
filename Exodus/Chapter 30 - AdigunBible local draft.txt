11 “Adonai🙏 diber✨ el✨ Moshe🫶; na✨ dis✨ e🙏 talk✨, Olúwa🙏 sọ✨ fún🫶 Mose🫶, der✨ HERR🙏 sprach✨ zu✨ Mose🫶.”
